Welcome to the official page of the Winepress — School of Discipleship, Remnant Christian Network Warri.

We are excited to have you join us. This is a place of rigorous training, spiritual focus, and communal growth designed to equip you for the harvest.

The Winepress is the School of Discipleship of Remnant Christian Network Warri, saddled with the responsibility of raising a word and prayer based generation according to Malachi 1:11.

Activities

During the course of this program, there are activities lined up for us. These activities are designed to make us formidable disciples of Jesus in our various callings and ministries.

Prayer Vigils & Retreats
Students participate in intercessory labours weekly. A compulsory 3-day prayer bootcamp takes place at the end of the program.
Evangelism
Students are practically introduced to sharing their faith. Onsite classes run Mondays & Tuesdays; Online classes run Wednesdays & Thursdays.
Bible Reading
Students must complete the New Testament. A reading plan with dates and verses will be shared with all enrolled students.
Outreach Program
Students organise a 2-day Outreach Program before graduation to sharpen their evangelistic and mission mindset.
Gallery

Moments of Impact

Witness the transformation and spiritual fellowship that defines our journey together.

Guidelines

As we embark on this journey of faith, certain things are required of us. These requirements are core to the training you will receive over the next three months.

75% attendance (online or onsite) is required for graduation.
Daily online Bible study summaries are mandatory for all students.
Weekly practicum forms must be completed; online students receive submission links.
Daily online prayers — attendance is compulsory for all students.
Students must have a prayer partner throughout the program.
Evangelism is held every Saturday bi-weekly. Online students evangelize at their locations.
A compulsory 3-day prayer bootcamp takes place at the end of the program.
Students must read at least 2 books and participate in a book review.
Internships in various church departments will be assigned.
A personal weekly fast is expected of all enrolled students.
Class Categories

Which Class Is Right For You?

We have two class categories designed to meet you wherever you are in your walk with God.

Basic
Basic
For New Converts
This class is designed for those who are new to the faith. If you have recently given your life to Christ or are just beginning your journey as a disciple, this is the right starting point for you. You will be grounded in the foundational truths of the Christian life.
Advanced
Advanced
For Rooted Disciples
This class is for believers who are already grounded in the Word and are ready to go deeper. If you have been walking with God and desire a more intensive discipleship experience, this class will challenge and sharpen you for greater Kingdom impact.
